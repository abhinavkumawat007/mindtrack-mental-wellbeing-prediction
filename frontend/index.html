1. Screen-to-Sleep Ratio Dominance

The engineered ratio between screen usage and sleep duration emerged as the #1 predictive factor (16.7% weight). Students whose daily phone time exceeded nightly sleep experienced steep declines in emotional resilience.

2. Strong Negative Correlation with Screen Time

Average daily usage demonstrates a high negative Pearson correlation (r = -0.82). Every additional hour above 5.0h daily corresponds to an average 0.22 point drop in estimated wellbeing.

3. Sleep as a Protective Resilience Buffer

Nightly sleep of 7.5+ hours (r = +0.77) serves as the strongest positive lifestyle factor, mitigating moderate stress levels and helping maintain stable mood equilibrium.

4. Phone Unlocks & Attention Fragmentation

High unlocking frequency (>180 checks/day) signals cognitive fragmentation, correlating directly with reported high stress independent of total hours spent.
